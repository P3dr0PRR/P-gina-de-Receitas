Coffee cupcake with whipped cream

The perfect cupcake to accompany your daily coffee. It's very easy and it turns out wonderful, it will definitely impress. Make it and then tell me what you think!

Time: 1h10
Yield: 12 servings
Difficulty: easy

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cup of warm water
  • 1 tablespoon of soluble coffee
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of oil
  • 2 cups of sugar
  • 2/3 cup of cocoa powder
  • 2 and 1/2 cups of wheat flour
  • 1 tablespoon of chemical baking powder
  • 2 cups of ready-made whipped cream
  • Coffee beans for decoration
  • 1 can of condensed milk
  • 1 tablespoon of soluble coffee
  • 1 tablespoon of cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons of butter

Preparation Method

In the blender, mix the water, soluble coffee, eggs, oil, sugar and cocoa powder until homogeneous. Transfer to a bowl, add the flour and baking powder and mix with a spoon. Pour into cupcake molds lined with paper cups and place in a large baking sheet, one next to the other. Bake in a preheated medium oven for 25 minutes or until done and golden. Remove and let cool.

For the filling, bring all ingredients to medium heat, stirring well until thickened. Turn off, let cool and place in a piping bag with a perle tip. Reserve. Place the whipped cream inside a piping bag with a pitanga tip and reserve.

For the assembly, make a hole in the center of the cupcakes and fill with the reserved coffee brigadeiro. Decorate the surfaces of the cupcakes with the reserved whipped cream, making circles. Decorate with coffee beans and leave in the refrigerator until serving time.
